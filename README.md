# Inmis Addon — Wear Realistic 3D Backpacks on Your Back

Inmis Addon 9minecraft Mod extends Inmis by replacing flat backpack icons with full 3D models on your character. Instead of showing a flat item sprite on your back, this mod adds a shaped pack with straps, a top carry handle, and a rolled flap. The pack bends and turns along with your body whenever you walk, sneak, or look around. It also lets you toggle your backpack screen open and closed using the exact same hotkey.

Replaces flat 2D back sprites with complete 3D backpack models.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Globox1997**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
